# 🧾 Sales Analysis Dashboard — Excel & Tableau Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance across different categories, segments, and regions using **Excel** for data exploration and **Tableau** for interactive dashboarding.  
The dataset contains detailed sales, profit, and customer information, allowing us to uncover key business insights and visualize trends.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Excel** – Data cleaning, pivot tables, top 10 analysis, slicers  
- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards, visual analytics, filtering

---

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset used is a sample **Superstore Sales Data**, which includes:
- Order details (Order ID, Date, Customer Name, Segment, Region)  
- Sales and Profit metrics  
- Product categories and subcategories  

> 📅 Period: Multiple years (used Month-Year for time series analysis)  
> 📝 Format: `.xlsx` file

---

## 📊 Excel Work
- Created Pivot Tables for:
  - **Top 10 Customers** by Sales
  - **Sales by Segment**
  - **Sales by Category**
- Added **Slicers** for dynamic filtering by `Region` and `Category`
- Built an interactive Excel dashboard with multiple charts controlled by slicers

---

## 📈 Tableau Dashboard

### 1. Sales & Profit by Category (Side-by-Side Bar Chart)
- **Dimension:** Category  
- **Measures:** Sales (Sum), Profit (Sum)  
- Visualization: Side-by-side bars to compare sales vs profit per category.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)
- **Dimension:** Order Date (Month-Year)  
- **Measure:** Sales (Sum)  
- Visualization: Trend line to analyze seasonality and growth patterns.

### 3. Top 10 Customers (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- **Dimension:** Customer Name  
- **Measure:** Sales (Sum)  
- Sorted descending with a **Top 10 filter** to highlight key customers.

### 4. Sales by Segment (Pie/Donut Chart)
- **Dimension:** Segment  
- **Measure:** Sales (Sum)  
- Visualization: Market share by segment.

### 5. Interactive Filters
- **Quick Filters for:**  
  - Region  
  - Category  
  - Segment  

All charts are combined into a single **interactive dashboard** with clean formatting, titles, and tooltips.

---

## 📝 Key Insights
- 🥇 **Technology** segment drives the **highest sales**, but **Office Supplies** often has better profit margins.  
- 📈 Sales show clear **monthly fluctuations**, peaking in certain quarters.  
- 👥 **Top 10 customers** account for a **significant share** of total sales.  
- 🌍 **East Region** has strong performance in both sales and profit compared to other regions.
